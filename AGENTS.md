# Zielonki roof constraints

- Preserve compliance of the main roof slopes with the applicable Zielonki MPZP. For this project's MN/MNU site in plan area 06, Resolution IX/55/2007, section 13(6)(4), requires symmetric gable or hipped main slopes of 37–45 degrees. See `knowledge-bank/zielonki/ZONING.md` for official sources and amendments; recheck the applicable plan before changing this constraint.
- The historical r44 house had 1.40 m attic knee walls and main gable pitches of approximately 40.134234 degrees. The resized front/rear-carport designs now use **44° for front-barn and 37.8710376967° for rear-barn**, with eaves **4.85 m** and a shared model ridge **7.7856938754 m**; this is a model datum, not height above terrain. When changing knee-wall height, move eaves and ridges together to preserve pitch unless the user requests a compliant pitch change. Check each main roof segment after geometry edits and data merges.
- Mandatory after EVERY change to building/storey/knee-wall height, roof height, roof footprint/span, pitch, ridge direction or roof junction, and after merging such edits: recompute each main slope angle and ridge elevation from the final geometry, not just the saved pitch label. For a symmetric gable use `ridge = eaves + tan(pitch) * perpendicularSpan / 2`. Verify each main slope is within **37–45°**, both sides of each gable are symmetric, and connected house ridges remain at the same elevation unless the user explicitly requests otherwise. Resizing a wing changes its ridge even when its pitch is unchanged; never assume preserving the old angle preserves the junction.
- Cached MPZP rule, verified **2026-09-11**: this site's **06.MNU.8**, area 06, **IX/55/2007 of 21 June 2007, §13 ust. 6 pkt 4, page 15**, requires symmetric gable/hipped main slopes **37–45°**. Official text: https://rastry.gison.pl/mpzp-public/zielonki/uchwaly/U_06_2007_55_IX.pdf#page=15 . Amendment **XIV/26/2020**, official item 1251/2020, does not change this slope clause; see the retained evidence in `knowledge-bank/zielonki/ZONING.md`. Use this recorded rule for routine geometry edits without repeating the web search. Recheck sources only if the site/plan changes, new amendment evidence appears, or the user asks to verify the law again.
- After roof edits, recompute the perpendicular ridge intersection from current footprints and verify the valley/junction is actually recognized. Inspect exterior AND interior: no detached ridge, hidden internal gable, duplicate roof sheet or roof face protruding through the other wing. Record final angles, ridge elevations and junction checks in the project's README. Equal ridge heights alone are insufficient; neither slope may fall outside 37–45° to achieve them.
- A compliant main-roof pitch does not establish compliance of the whole building or permission for the flat garage terrace/canopy. Keep those separate questions explicit as documented in the zoning evidence.

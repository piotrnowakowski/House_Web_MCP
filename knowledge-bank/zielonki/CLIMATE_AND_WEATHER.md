Annual preset totals are 679 mm precipitation, 1,540 sunshine hours and 654 mm ET₀. The app's `18 mm` irrigation setting is a configurable modeled monthly input, not observed rainfall.

## Design implications

- Expect a real freeze-thaw season and select reliably hardy plants.
- Summer ET₀ peaks in June–August, so new planting still needs establishment watering even when species are labeled low-water.
- Heavy ground and shallow groundwater mean water availability is governed by drainage and root-zone oxygen, not rainfall totals alone.
- Seasonal outputs are comparative estimates; they do not replace shade measurement, microclimate observation or irrigation design.
